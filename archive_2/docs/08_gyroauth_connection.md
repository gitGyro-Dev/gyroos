# GyroAuth Connection

GyroAuth is an application of Gyro Logic to dynamic identity and authentication.

---

## Authentication as Convergence

Authentication is defined as convergence of behavioral and structural trajectories under repeated observation.

Instead of matching static credentials, GyroAuth evaluates whether a trajectory converges to a stable structure.

---

## Identity as Soliton

A valid identity corresponds to a soliton-like trajectory that:

- maintains structural coherence
- preserves phase consistency
- re-converges after perturbation

---

## Stability Score

Authentication is based on a Stability score derived from:

- structural invariance
- phase coherence
- re-convergence
- temporal consistency

---

## Continuous Authentication

Identity is not verified once, but continuously evaluated as stability over time.

---

## Security Model

Adversarial behavior is modeled as pseudo-soliton formation:

- locally stable
- globally unstable
- collapses under multi-scale observation

This allows detection of spoofing and behavioral attacks.
